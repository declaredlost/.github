**A sidescrolling multiplayer action RPG, built with Bevy.**

[**declaredlost.com**](https://declaredlost.com)

Declared Lost is in early development. Follow along on the
[devlog](https://declaredlost.com/devlog/).

## Bevy extensions

The game's Bevy extensions, independent of it and open source under
MIT OR Apache-2.0:

| Repository | Build | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [bevy_mettle](https://github.com/declaredlost/bevy_mettle) | [![bevy_mettle](https://img.shields.io/github/actions/workflow/status/declaredlost/bevy_mettle/build.yml?branch=main&label=build&style=flat-square)](https://github.com/declaredlost/bevy_mettle/actions/workflows/build.yml) | A gameplay framework in the spirit of Unreal's Gameplay Ability System: attributes, effects and abilities, with prediction over lightyear. |
| [bevy_arbor](https://github.com/declaredlost/bevy_arbor) | [![bevy_arbor](https://img.shields.io/github/actions/workflow/status/declaredlost/bevy_arbor/build.yml?branch=main&label=build&style=flat-square)](https://github.com/declaredlost/bevy_arbor/actions/workflows/build.yml) | Reactive behaviour trees: guards re-checked every tick, tasks as entities. |
| [bevy_harness](https://github.com/declaredlost/bevy_harness) | [![bevy_harness](https://img.shields.io/github/actions/workflow/status/declaredlost/bevy_harness/build.yml?branch=main&label=build&style=flat-square)](https://github.com/declaredlost/bevy_harness/actions/workflows/build.yml) | Testing Bevy apps: the real app, headless, stepped tick by tick, and asserted on. |
